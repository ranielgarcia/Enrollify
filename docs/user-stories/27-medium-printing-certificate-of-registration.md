## Summary

Generate a PDF CoR showing student info, enrolled subjects, schedules, teacher names, and fees summary.

## Persona(s)

- Student (self-service), Admissions Staff (on request)

## Acceptance Criteria

1. CoR available when enrollment status is Enrolled and assessment exists.
2. Includes subjects, schedule, teacher, fees per subject/misc, and totals.
