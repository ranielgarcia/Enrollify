## Summary

Parents can view student grades, attendance (future), and billing/payment status; receive notifications.

## Persona(s)

- Parent/Guardian

## User Story

As a Parent, I want to view my student's grades and payment status, so I can support their academic progress.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. View grades once published; view balances and payment history summary per term.
2. Notifications (email/SMS ready) when grades are posted or balances due (optional).
