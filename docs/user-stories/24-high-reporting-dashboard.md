## Summary

Provide administrative reports: students per year/course/subject, totals per subject (capacity <= 40), finance summaries, academic performance.

## Persona(s)

- Registrar, Cashier/Finance Officer, Department Head/Dean

## User Story

As a Registrar, I want configurable reports and exports (Excel/PDF), so I can monitor enrollment and capacities.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Filters by term, college, course, section, subject, teacher.
2. Export to Excel and PDF; totals and capacity usage indicators.
3. Students-per-subject report shows max capacity and current count.
