## Glossary

- Regular Student: Enrolls by section; subjects auto-selected from section offerings.
- Irregular Student: Enrolls per subject offering manually; may cross year levels or colleges.
- Transferee: Student transferring with credit evaluation; uses Equivalent Subject mappings.
- Offering: A scheduled instance of a subject tied to a section, teacher, and room.
- CoR: Certificate of Registration; official list of enrolled subjects and schedule per term.
- Assessment: Computed charges (tuition + misc) for a term.
- Clearance: Finance flag that allows enrollment despite balances.
